Legal flags for your lawyer

  • Decided: the director gets his 10% as cuotas at incorporation, with a partners' agreement (pacto de socios) that changes it to 3% plus RD$300,000 a month at month 37. An SRL cannot issue bonds (Ley 479-08, Art. 91), so the lawyer writes it this way.
  • An SRL needs at least 2 partners (Art. 89), and cuotas must be fully paid at formation; work does not count as a contribution (Art. 92).
  • An unpaid director who works full time could be treated as an employee. Put the arrangement in writing.
  • The loan is in dollars at 6%, secured by the founder. Ask the bank for its full cost sheet (opening fee, credit life insurance, legal fees) before signing.
